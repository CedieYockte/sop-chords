SOP

Live chords for any YouTube song
  1. Open a song on YouTube.
  2. Press Start listening on the SOP panel.
  3. Play the song — chords, key and capo appear live.
  4. Play it through once and SOP saves a chord chart you can scrub, transpose and copy.
Chords are detected from the audio on your own computer — nothing is uploaded. Clean worship recordings work best; dense mixes may need a quick check by ear.
